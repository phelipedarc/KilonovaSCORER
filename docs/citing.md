## Simulations

The simulation grid is on Zenodo (CC BY 4.0): <https://zenodo.org/records/19824266>

The two-component model comes from Villar et al. (2017) and is run with [Redback](https://redback.readthedocs.io) (Sarin et al. 2024). Please cite them too if you generate your own simulations.
